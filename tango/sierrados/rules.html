THE COVERT OPERATIONS DIRECTIVE

The Covert Operations exercise will test your ability to operate under uncertainty, maintain discretion, exercise sound judgment, protect information, and accomplish objectives through teamwork and discipline.

OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES

DIRECTIVE // 01

Silence Is Security

What happens during the operation stays within the operation. Do not disclose mission details, identities, instructions, or outcomes to unauthorized individuals.

DIRECTIVE // 02

Trust Is Earned

Not everyone possesses the same information. Share only what is necessary with those authorized to receive it.

DIRECTIVE // 03

Observe More, Reveal Less

Be attentive to details. Listen carefully, think critically, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of your intentions, observations, or discoveries.

DIRECTIVE // 04

Leave No Digital Footprint

Do not photograph, record, screenshot, reproduce, or distribute operational materials without explicit authorization.

DIRECTIVE // 05

Your Team Is Your Responsibility

Coordinate with your assigned members. Protect the integrity of your team, fulfill your designated responsibilities, and never compromise another participant.

DIRECTIVE // 06

Question Everything, Verify Everything

Do not mistake assumptions for facts. Evaluate information, identify inconsistencies, and verify evidence before acting.

DIRECTIVE // 07

The Mission Has Boundaries

All activities must remain within the authorized training exercise. Unauthorized access, intrusion, harassment, trespassing, and interference with real-world operations are prohibited.

DIRECTIVE // 08

Integrity Above Victory

Deception may be part of a simulated scenario, but dishonesty toward facilitators, fabrication of evidence, cheating, and violations of established rules are unacceptable.

DIRECTIVE // 09

Safety Overrides Secrecy

Report safety concerns, misconduct, privacy breaches, and emergencies immediately to the designated authorities. Confidentiality must never prevent legitimate reporting.

DIRECTIVE // 10

When the Mission Ends, the Silence Remains

Do not reveal operational scenarios, solutions, or protected information after completing the exercise unless officially authorized.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

THE MISSION MAY END.
YOUR RESPONSIBILITY DOES NOT.

Maintain the standards of operational discipline throughout and beyond the exercise.

DISCIPLINE DISCRETION INTEGRITY
Observe. Analyze. Adapt. Execute.
